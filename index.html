Turn your Spotify playlists into a guessing game!

Add a public Spotify playlist, listen to a short clip from one of its songs, and see if you can name both the song and the artist.

How to play

  1. Pick a difficulty level.
  2. Copy the URL of a public Spotify playlist.
  3. Paste the URL in the input field above.
  4. Click Submit to start the quiz.
  5. Listen to the clip and try to guess the song and artist.

Classic mode

In Classic mode, you will hear a 5-second clip from the playlist. Guess the song title and artist. If you get it right, you move on to the next song. If you get it wrong, you can try again or skip.

Easy mode

In Easy mode, you will hear a 7-second clip and choose the song and artist from a list of options. This mode is not connected yet.
