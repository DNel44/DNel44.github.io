Turn your spotify playlists into a guessing game!

Add a public Spotify playlist, listen to a short clip from one of its songs, and see if you can name both the song and the artist.

How to play

  1. Pick your difficulty level from classic to easy
  2. Copy the URL of a public Spotify playlist
  3. Paste the URL in the input field above
  4. Click "Submit" to start the quiz
  5. Listen to the short clip and try to guess the song and artist

Classic mode

In classic mode, you will hear a 5-second clip of a song from the playlist. You will have to guess both the song title and the artist. If you get it right, you move on to the next song. If you get it wrong, you can try again or skip to the next song.

Easy mode

In easy mode, you will hear a 7-second clip of a song from the playlist. You will have to guess both the song title and the artist from a list of options. If you get it right, you move on to the next song. If you get it wrong, you can try again or skip to the next song.
