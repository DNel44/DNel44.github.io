Turn your YouTube playlists into a guessing game!

Add a public YouTube playlist, play a short clip, and guess the song and artist.

Difficulty Levels

Please paste the playlist URL

Videos play in YouTube's official embedded player. Each short clip starts at a random point and lasts 5 seconds in Classic or 7 seconds in Easy.

How to play

  1. Pick a difficulty level.
  2. Copy the URL of a public YouTube playlist.
  3. Paste the URL in the input field above.
  4. Click Start quiz to open the selected mode.
  5. Play the randomly selected short clip and guess using the playlist video title and channel name.

Classic mode

In Classic mode, guess the title and channel name for each playlist video. Get both right for 1 point, or one right for 0.5 points. Incorrect answers and skipped questions earn 0 points.

Easy mode

In Easy mode, choose the title and channel name from a list of options. A correct answer earns 1 point; incorrect answers and skipped questions earn 0 points.
